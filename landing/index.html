What it does

Drop a photo in, pick a size (2x, 4x, 8x, or 16x), and MaxRes uses AI to enlarge it into a crisp, print-ready image — saved right next to your original. Everything happens on your own computer.

Your photos stay private

MaxRes has no network access at all. There are no uploads, no accounts, and no tracking. It's the perfect tool for restoring personal and family photos you'd never want to send to a website.

First launch: security warning

MaxRes is free and open-source, so its installers aren't code-signed (certificates cost money we don't charge you for). Your system may warn that the app is from an "unidentified developer" — this is expected. To open it:

  • Windows: click More info → Run anyway
  • macOS: right-click the app → Open → Open
